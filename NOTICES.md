# Third-party components

Nothing in this list is redistributed here, with one declared exception: the silent demo clips in
`media/`, adapted from RAVDESS and credited in their own section at the end of this file. Each
component is named because the code calls it, measures it, or was run on it, and each keeps its
own licence. Where a licence is stated below it was read from the source named; where it is not,
check the project's own repository before relying on it.

## Speech corpora — no audio is redistributed

| corpus | used for | licence |
|---|---|---|
| RAVDESS | the same-actor human reference, and the English ladder source | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (Livingstone & Russo 2018; Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.1188976`) |
| IEMOCAP | training and held-out testing of the pre-registered layer | licence-gated: an electronic release form must be signed before download |
| ESD (speaker 0002) | the Mandarin ladder source | licence-gated: a signed licence is required; research use only |
| JVNV (F2) | the Japanese ladder source | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

IEMOCAP and ESD are licence-gated and may not be redistributed. RAVDESS (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and
JVNV (CC BY-SA 4.0) do permit redistribution, with attribution and under the same licence —
RAVDESS for non-commercial use only. Even so, **no corpus audio and no derivative audio is
included here**: one rule covers every stimulus, and the source releases are the right place to
obtain the originals. `code/ladder/` regenerates every manipulated file from the sources once you
hold them. The one RAVDESS derivative that is included is the silent demo material in `media/`,
credited below.

## Rendering

Facial animation was rendered on digital characters in Unreal Engine 5.6 under the standard
Unreal Engine EULA. **No stimulus video and no animation-curve array is redistributed here**; the
silent demo clips in `media/` are the only rendered frames, and are covered in their own section.
The scoring code in `code/audit/` reads such arrays and is included; the arrays themselves are
not, and are regenerated by running the models.

## Perception data in `data/perception/`

De-identified responses of this study's own participants, who took part with informed consent.
Recruitment-platform IDs, usernames, session IDs, IP-derived codes, timestamps, age, gender and
interface language are not included, and participant codes are assigned at random within each
listener group.

## Demo clips in `media/` — credit and licence

`media/e_vs_h.mp4`, `media/e_vs_h.gif`, `media/brow_difference.gif` and
`media/brow_difference_peak.png` are silent renders of an EmoFace avatar driven by a RAVDESS
actor's audio — actor 08, angry (cell `rav08_ang`). The E side is the model's output at its
shipped settings; the H side is the same render with the actor's own brow motion retargeted onto
it; the difference images are `|H - E|`. The renders were made in Unreal Engine 5.6. No audio is
included, and the frames are cropped to the upper face.

**Credit:** RAVDESS (Livingstone & Russo, 2018), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

(RAVDESS). PLoS ONE 13(5): e0196391. Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.1188976`.
